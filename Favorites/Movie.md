**My Favorite Movies or Series.**

1. The Dark Knight. 
2. Final Destination
3. Real steel
4. The Fast and the Furious Franchise. 
5. Space Jam

1. The Dark Knight — Batman, Lieutenant Jim Gordon, and District Attorney Harvey Dent ally to dismantle organized crime in Gotham City, but a psychopathic criminal mastermind known as The Joker (Heath Ledger) descends on the city, plunging it into chaos and testing the limits of Batman's moral code. 

2. Final Destination — High school student Alex Browning has a premonition of a plane explosion and, along with a few classmates, leaves the flight before it blows up.  But by cheating death, they've disrupted its plan — and Death now hunts them down one by one in increasingly elaborate, Rube Goldberg-style accidents. 

3. Real Steel — In a near-future where robot boxing has replaced human boxing, a washed-up former boxer and struggling promoter, Charlie Kenton (Hugh Jackman), reluctantly teams up with his estranged son Max to train a discarded, obsolete robot named Atom for a shot at the championship. 

4. The Fast and the Furious Franchise — An action franchise centered on street racing, heists, and family.  It follows Dominic Toretto (Vin Diesel) and Brian O'Conner (Paul Walker), who go from rivals to allies, evolving from underground street racers in Los Angeles to a global team taking down drug lords, corrupt governments, and eventually threats to the entire world.

5. Space Jam — The Looney Tunes are abducted by aliens called the Nerdlucks to become attractions at their failing amusement park, Moron Mountain.  The Tunes challenge the aliens to a basketball game to win their freedom, but the Nerdlucks steal the talents of NBA stars to become the monstrous "Monstars." The Tunes recruit retired basketball legend Michael Jordan to help them win.
